# About Tangled Taste

Tangled Taste is a personal project that aims to visualize the relationships between cuisines, dishes, and ingredients from around the world.

The site is currently in early stages and I welcome any feedback. The site was created using Claude Desktop (Opus 5.5 and Sonnet 5.5 models).

If you notice anything inaccurate about ingredients in a particular dish, please let me know. I am aiming for directional correctness for the sake of simple visualization rather than precise technical correctness.   

I'm neither a web developer, nor a chef, nor an illustrator. I just enjoy data visualizations and wanted to create something light and fun. I hope you enjoy!
